    About Us

    National Board Of Computer Education

    Established to bridge the digital skills gap with industry-aligned computer courses, transparent assessment, and accessible training centres in urban and rural areas.

    Our Mission

    Deliver practical, job-ready computer education and digital fluency to learners at affordable fees.

    Our Vision

    Build a trusted network of study centres and certified professionals powering modern workplaces.

    Quality & Verification

    Structured curriculum, practical lab evaluations, and instant online certificate & student verification.
