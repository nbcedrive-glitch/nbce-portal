    About Us

    National Board Of Computer Education

    Established to advance computer literacy, vocational skill training, and standardized assessment across India through authorized study centres.

    Our Mission

    Deliver practical IT and vocational computer training accessible to students across cities and towns.

    Our Vision

    Build a trusted national network of skill centres and certified professionals for modern industry.

    Authentic Certification

    Transparent lab evaluations, standardized curriculum, and instant online student & certificate verification.
