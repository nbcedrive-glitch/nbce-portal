About NBCE

Bridging the Digital Divide Through Accessible Computer Literacy

National Board Of Computer Education (NBCE) is an autonomous national-level IT education and skill development board committed to delivering affordable, job-oriented computer education to students in every corner of the country.

Registered under Govt. norms and certified under ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management Systems, NBCE empowers affiliated training centres with standardized syllabi, online examination portals, and QR-verifiable certificates valid for private and semi-government sectors.

Our Mission

To make modern IT & vocational education affordable and reachable for rural and urban youth alike.

Our Vision

To build a digitally skilled workforce capable of excelling in global IT, accounting, and software industries.
